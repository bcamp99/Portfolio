It rolled in on a random Wednesday,  
from across the Pacific coast  
his want for her,  
a message in a bottle.  

They say you don’t realize what you have  
until it’s gone.  
But she didn’t realize  
what she could have had  
until he wanted it from her.  

Like the swell of an ocean  
before a storm,  
her longing grew.  
And as the waves rose high enough  
to drown her,  
his tide receded back to sea.  

How could they be drifting  
through different oceans  
when he’d come to her  
from this very one  
only months before?  

Like she always did,  
she mistook the shimmer  
on the water’s surface  
for something that would remain  
after the tide changed.  

But the sea has always been good  
at selling illusions.  

And as the sun slipped  
beneath the horizon,  
the light that made them beautiful  
disappeared.  

She was left wanting something  
that was never meant to be gone  
before they ever had the chance  
to reach for it.  

She would’ve sent him  
a hundred messages in bottles,  
each one holding everything  
she never got the chance  
to show him.  

Instead, she stood  
at the edge of the cliffs,  
watching what could’ve been  
fade beyond the ocean’s horizon,  

until he became  
just another message in a bottle  
the tide carried somewhere else.
