She dances beneath the pulsing lights.

But she dreams of dancing  
in the muted yellow hue of a kitchen,  
of arms around her waist  
as she cooks,  
a life she’s yet to know  
but somehow misses.

On this floor,  
she’s just another body.

The lover girl inside  
is nowhere to be seen  
as the beat grows louder,  
deafening something  
between a memory and a dream  
for just a few hours.

The movement lets her forget  
that all she’s ever desired  
was to be truly loved.

Not wanted  
the way they devour her with their eyes.

She gives in to the male games  
because she’s learned their dance.

She knows the steps—  
the smile,  
the glance across the room,  
the way desire is mistaken for connection.

Because that’s all they’ve ever wanted:  
to take,  
to consume,  
to leave with a body,  
never a heart.

Still, beneath the pulsing lights,  
she catches herself dreaming  
of another life.

One where she’s dancing  
in the yellow hue of a kitchen,  
his hands around her waist  
as they laugh through the words  
to their favorite song.

In a perfect life,  
a little girl’s voice  
would stumble into the chorus,  
singing words  
she doesn’t quite know yet.

But not tonight.

Tonight,  
she belongs to the pulsing lights.

To the beat.

To the male games.

And for a little while,  
she lets herself become  
the girl the room wants her to be,

while quietly yearning  
for the lover girl buried within  
to finally be loved.
